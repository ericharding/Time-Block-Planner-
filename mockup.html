UI Interaction Features:

  • Drag-Select: Click and drag on empty space to create a new time block
  • Drag-Drop: Click and drag existing blocks to move them to different times
  • Edit: Click on a block to edit its name, duration, and other properties
  • Resize: Drag the bottom edge of a block to adjust its duration
  • Mark Interruption: Click the interruption button to create a new column starting at the current time
  • Cross-Column Drag: Drag blocks from the original plan to the revised plan to reschedule
  • Time Range: Adjust start/end times to show only relevant hours (e.g., 8 AM - 5 PM)
